Questions people ask

Is Folkbranch really free?

Yes. It is open-source family tree software under the AGPL-3.0 licence, with no paid plan. If you want relatives to reach it from anywhere, you pay only for the server you choose. The cloud assistant and speech services are optional and use your own key.

Where is my family’s data stored?

On your computer or your server, in one SQLite database and a folder of photos. Nothing is sent to us. The server makes a copy of the database every day, and you can download a full copy at any time.

Do my relatives need to install anything?

No. They open a link in the browser on a phone or a computer. To answer a question by voice they do not even need an account.

Can I bring my tree from Ancestry, MyHeritage or Gramps?

Yes. Export a GEDCOM file there and import it here; GEDCOM 5.5.1 and 7.0 are supported, as are Excel, CSV and plain text. Photos can come along with the file.

Does the AI see my family’s data?

Faces and speech are handled on your own device. The cloud assistant is off until you add a key; once on, what it reads goes to the provider you picked, or stays home with a local model. Its edits wait for your confirmation.

What happens if the project stops?

Your archive keeps working. The code is open, the data is a standard SQLite file, and GEDCOM exports open in any family tree program.

Do I need my own server or technical skills?

No. Your AI installs it on the computer you already have, and you open it in the browser. A server is only needed if relatives should reach it from anywhere; your AI can set that up too.

Does it run on Windows?

The one-line install is tested on Mac and Linux. On Windows or a server, it runs in Docker; your AI can set that up for you.
